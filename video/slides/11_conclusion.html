What the project proves.

Closed loop

Measurements update hidden states. Alarms explain evidence. What-if runs the same physics forward.

Reproducible

One command starts the stack; 13 tests and an evaluation suite produce the numbers shown here.

No truth leakage

Plant parameters and truth stay out of the twin process and out of MQTT.

Honest limits

Simulation only · cells treated as identical · 1RC weak at low SoC/low temperature · thresholds calibrated on one duty cycle · small sensor offsets are hard to distinguish · SQLite is single-asset scale.

The model/twin mismatch is intentional. It makes the validation a real test rather than a tautology.

python scripts/run_all.py --freshreports/results.md
